# Getting Started and Progression

Crystal Nexus is a tech and energy mod built around crystals, machines, FE transfer, ore processing, and big late-game builds. Early on, you will work with Ancient Crystal materials. Later, the mod opens up into Blutonium, Invertium, Polymer, EE-Matter, singularities, reactors, and Zero Point power.

## Early Goals

### Ancient Crystal

Mine Ancient Crystal Ore to begin the material progression path.

- Use Ancient Crystal with Iron Ingots in the Crystal Purifier to create Crystalized Alloy.
- Crystalized Alloy unlocks early machine frames, processing machines, and tools.

### Crystal Purifier

The Crystal Purifier combines one Ancient Crystal and one Iron Ingot into two Crystalized Alloy Ingots. It can also process Bone Blocks into Nitrile.

## First Machine Chain

A practical early machine setup is:

1. Energy Generator for FE generation.
2. Battery Cell or another energy-capable block for buffering.
3. Basic Energy Cable, Energy Cable, or Energy Cable Mk 2 to move FE.
4. Crystal Ore Crusher for raw ore to dust.
5. Crystal Dust Separator for dust to nuggets.
6. Crystal Smelter or Iron Smelter for smelting.
7. Conveyor Belts for basic automation.
