# Resources and Materials

This page groups the major resources used across machine and item progression.

Used as a foundation material for the crystal technology chain.

- Crystalized Alloy Ingot

## Metal Forming and Machine Parts

The Parts Assembler produces common formed-metal components from ingots:

- Sheets: Iron, Copper, Gold, and Titanium. These items use the common `c:plates/<material>` tags for recipe compatibility.
- Rods: Iron, Copper, Gold, and Titanium. These items use `c:rods` and the material-specific `c:rods/<material>` tags.
- Bolts: Iron bolts use the common `c:bolts` tags.

Electromechanical parts include:

- Stator
- Heating Coil
- Iron Bearing and Titanium Bearing
- Electric Motor and Titanium Electric Motor

These parts are used in machine construction and are grouped together in the Materials & Parts creative tab. Check JEI for their current crafting recipes.

## Blutonium

Forms:

- Blutonium Ore
- Raw Blutonium
- Blutonium Dust
- Blutonium Nugget
- Blutonium Ingot
- Blutonium Block
- Pure Blutonium
- Blutonium Waste

Important notes:

- Raw Blutonium and Blutonium Ingots are radioactive.
- The Hazmat enchantment negates the radioactive effect.
- Pure Blutonium is 75% more efficient than normal Blutonium.
- Blutonium Waste is associated with reactor waste output and waste recycling.

## Singularity Items

Singularities are powerful late-game items:

- Iron Singularity: provides Resistance.
- Diamond Singularity: makes tools in inventory unbreakable.
- Gold Singularity: good luck charm.
- Copper Singularity: right-click to age blocks and crops; sneak + right-click to de-age.
- Redstone Singularity: check JEI/in-game use.
- Quartz Singularity: protects from hostile mobs.
- Coal Singularity: infinite fuel source and works for reactors.
- Energy Singularity: FE-related singularity.

They are created through the Singularity Compressor and Matter Matrix chain.
